How long has IBM been around?

Hold up your A, B, C or D card

IBM started in 1911. What it builds today looks nothing like what it built back then. Technology changes, and companies have to change with it.

Let them vote with cards. Press R or click Reveal. "IBM has been around for more than a century, but the technology IBM works on today is very different from decades ago." Plant the idea: technology changes, companies change with it — careers too.

IBM or not IBM?

IBMthumbs upNot IBMthumbs downTap a card to flip it
Show each card, students vote thumbs up (IBM) or down (not IBM), then flip. Twist cards: the first smartphone (IBM Simon, 1994) and the floppy disk (why the save icon looks like that). Mainframes are also IBM (System/360, 1964) if you want a bonus. Takeaway: "IBM isn't just one technology. IBM has changed what it does many times." Bridge: "And careers can change in a similar way."
